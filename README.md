# EduSchool-Saathi | Smart School Management Software & School ERP

**Har School Ka Saathi — Building a Smarter, Digitally Connected Education Ecosystem.**

EduSchool-Saathi is an EdTech initiative based in **Madhubani, Bihar, India**, focused on simplifying school administration through digital school management software and School ERP solutions.

We aim to help schools organize student information, attendance, fee records, examinations, homework, timetables, and parent-teacher communication through a centralized digital platform.

📍 **Location:** Bara Bazar, Madhubani, Bihar, India  
🏷️ **Industry:** Education Technology (EdTech) | SaaS | School ERP  
🎯 **Mission:** Make digital school management more accessible and practical for schools, starting from Bihar.

---

## 🚀 What Is EduSchool-Saathi?

EduSchool-Saathi is being built around a simple idea: school administration should be more organized, accessible, and less dependent on repetitive paperwork.

Our school management platform aims to connect school administrators, teachers, students, and parents in one digital ecosystem.

### 🎓 School Management Solutions

- **Student Management:** Student profiles, admissions, and academic records.
- **Attendance Management:** Digital attendance records for students and teachers.
- **Fee Management:** Fee records, dues tracking, and payment information.
- **Examination & Results:** Exam schedules, marks, and academic results.
- **Homework & Timetable:** Support for academic schedules and assignments.
- **Parent-Teacher Communication:** School notices, announcements, and updates.
- **Reports & Administration:** Organized information to support daily school operations.

*Feature availability may vary as the platform evolves. Contact us to confirm current capabilities.*

## 💡 Our Vision

To contribute to a connected education ecosystem where schools can adopt practical technology, teachers can manage academic activities more efficiently, and parents can stay informed about their children's education.

We aim to support digital transformation for schools in **Madhubani, Darbhanga, and other districts of Bihar**, with the potential to serve schools across India.
