## 🏫 About EduSchool-Saathi

**EduSchool-Saathi** is an Education Technology (EdTech) initiative based in **Bara Bazar, Madhubani, Bihar, India**, focused on making school administration simpler, smarter, and more digitally connected.

Our vision is to help schools manage their everyday academic and administrative activities through an integrated **School Management Software and School ERP platform**.

From student information and attendance to fee records, examinations, homework, and parent-teacher communication, our goal is to bring essential school operations together in one organized digital environment.

We aim to support schools in Madhubani, Darbhanga, and other districts of Bihar as they explore digital transformation.

**Our brand promise:** Technology that helps schools focus more on education and less on repetitive administrative work.

> 🎯 Our mission is simple: make digital school management more accessible, practical, and affordable for schools.

## 💡 The Problem We Aim to Solve

School administrators and teachers often have to manage multiple responsibilities using registers, spreadsheets, paper records, and separate communication channels.

These workflows can create challenges such as:

- Time-consuming administrative work.
- Difficulty maintaining organized student records.
- Manual attendance and fee tracking.
- Scattered examination and academic information.
- Communication gaps between schools and parents.
- Limited access to consolidated administrative reports.

EduSchool-Saathi aims to address these challenges through practical, technology-driven school management solutions.

## 🔐 Our Approach to Data Security

School platforms can process sensitive information relating to students, parents, teachers, and staff. Data protection must therefore be an essential part of software development.

Our security priorities include:

- Role-based access to information.
- Separation of data between participating schools.
- Secure authentication and protected connections.
- Database backup and recovery planning.
- Responsible handling of personal information.
- Regular maintenance and security improvements.

Security measures depend on the deployed product architecture and configuration. Schools should review the applicable privacy policy and security arrangements before onboarding.
